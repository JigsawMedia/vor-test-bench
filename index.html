Where the value sits

The spread between best and worst on the favourite is 0.15, which is 7.9 percent of the price. On Newcastle it is 6.00, which is 15.8 percent. The further down the market you go, the more the book you pick matters, and the less anyone shops around.

Common questions

When does this market settle?
On the final league table after 38 games. Books differ on abandoned seasons; check the rules at your book.
Why do books disagree by this much?
Outrights carry a wider margin than match odds because the liability sits open for nine months.
